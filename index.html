Recent years and predictions

  • 2026 is a true prediction, because 2025's ocean conditions are already known. 2027–2028 are scenarios: the model predicts each year's kelp from the previous year's temperature and upwelling, so you pick what the ocean is like in 2026–2027.
  • Ranges show the model's typical error: predictions are usually within about 3.5× of the real value. In testing, the model beat simply guessing the average the majority of the time.
  • Since 2023, kelp has been lower than the model expected, so these predictions may be on the high side. The model shows a pattern.
  • Data: Kelpwatch (Landsat kelp canopy), SIO Shore Stations Program (Scripps Pier temperature), NOAA CUTI upwelling index.
